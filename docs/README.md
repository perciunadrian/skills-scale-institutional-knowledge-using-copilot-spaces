# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory is the central index for OctoAcme's project management processes. Use these guides to understand how work moves from a new idea through delivery, release, and continuous improvement.

## Project management process overview

OctoAcme projects follow a lightweight lifecycle of initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Initiation confirms the problem, SMART goals, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, and initial risks before work is authorized. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria, estimates, a Definition of Done, dependencies, milestones, and a test approach.

During execution, delivery teams use a project board and small pull requests, with CI tests and linting, peer review, manual QA when needed, and regular demos to maintain quality and transparency. Named Project and Product Managers coordinate delivery and outcomes; developers implement and test changes; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide inputs and approvals. Risks and dependencies are maintained in a shared register and escalated through team-level triage, PM and Product Lead coordination, and sponsor or incident leadership when necessary.

Communication is structured around standups, weekly delivery and PM/Product syncs, milestone demonstrations, stakeholder updates, and documented escalation paths. Releases require passing CI and security checks, acceptance criteria, release notes, smoke tests, rollback planning, and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives then capture successes, improvements, owners, and due dates so lessons become tracked backlog or issue actions for the next iteration.
